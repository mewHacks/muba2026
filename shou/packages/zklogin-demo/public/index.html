How SHOU Works — All 5 Phases

        
From setup to scammer blocked — the full flow.

        
      

      


        

          

            1
            Setup (once, while calm)
          

          

            
    
              
  • Son installs the extension on Mom's laptop.
    • 
              
  • Mom signs in with Google. A wallet is created automatically. No seed phrase to lose or give away.
    • 
              
  • They set spending rules together:
                
      
                  
    • Under $1 → goes through instantly.
      • 
                  
    • $1 – $5 → 2-minute cooldown.
      • 
                  
    • Above $5 or any scam → son must approve.
      • 
                
    
              
    • 
              
  • Recovery net: If Mom's Google account is ever lost, her son + one relative can recover the funds together.
    • 
            

          

        


        

          

            2
            She chats normally
          

          

            
    
              
  • She opens WhatsApp, Messenger, or Facebook. Nothing changes. No buttons to press, no copy-pasting.
    • 
              
  • The extension reads the chat quietly in the background.
    • 
              
  • AI scores each message inside a secure enclave. Personal data is stripped before scoring. It checks for: fake urgency, fake authority ("I'm from the police"), and secrecy demands ("don't tell your son").
    • 
              
  • A badge appears next to the chat:
                
      
                  
    • 🟢 Green — Safe, normal chat.
      • 
                  
    • 🟠 Amber — Be careful.
      • 
                  
    • 🔴 Red — Scam detected.
      • 
                
    
              
    • 
            

          

        


        

          

            3
            She tries to send money
          

          

            
Scammers say "the app is lying, just send it." This is where SHOU takes over.

            
    
              
  • Mom clicks "Send". The wallet checks with the enclave first.
    • 
              
  • The enclave signs a risk score. It remembers the chat was flagged as a scam.
    • 
              
  • The transaction goes to the Sui chain with that risk score attached.
    • 
            

          

        


        

          

            4
            The chain locks the money
          

          

            
    
              
  • The contract sees HIGH risk and refuses to release the funds.
    • 
              
  • Money goes into escrow instead of reaching the scammer.
    • 
              
  • The scammer gets nothing.
    • 
            

          

        


        

          

            5
            Son blocks & refunds
          

          

            
    
              
  • Son gets an alert on his dashboard: who, how much, and why it was flagged.
    • 
              
  • Son clicks "Block & Refund." The money goes straight back to Mom. He can never redirect it to himself.
    • 
              
  • Scammer's address is denylisted so they can't target others.
